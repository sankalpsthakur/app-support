# Study Munch Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 11, 2026

Study Munch helps you create and follow learning paths from topics, notes, documents, and practice material. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## Information You Provide

Cram may process learning topics, notes, uploaded documents, flashcards, progress, and account details when you choose to use account-backed or cloud-powered features.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used to create study paths, generate lessons and flashcards, save progress, support streaks and reminders, and operate the service.

## Documents and Study Material

Documents you upload are used only to create and improve your personal study experience. Do not upload material you do not have permission to use.

## Third-Party Services

Cram may use Apple platform services, authentication providers, backend hosting, analytics, crash reporting, and AI processing providers where needed to operate app features.

## Children

Cram is intended for general learning use. If a school, parent, or guardian manages use for a minor, they are responsible for supervising uploaded material and account use.

## Data Deletion

You may request deletion of account-backed data by contacting support. Local data can be removed by deleting the app from your device.
