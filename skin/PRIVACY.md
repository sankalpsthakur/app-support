# MoleMap Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 30, 2026

MoleMap is designed as a private, local-only skin photo journal for personal documentation.

In the current build, photos, notes, spot records, scan timestamps, measurements, and preferences stay on the user's device. The app is intended to work without internet access. It has no account system, server sync, analytics SDK, cloud upload, clinic workflow, visit workflow, or advertising tracker.

MoleMap requests camera access so users can capture guided body-region photos. MoleMap also requests read access to the photo library only when the user chooses to import an existing image during a scan, so the selected image can be copied into the user's private local record. MoleMap does not save photos back to the photo library. Imported images are stored inside the app on the device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Users can create local exports from Settings, such as CSV, ZIP backup, or clinician PDF where available. Export files stay on the device until the user chooses a destination through the iOS share sheet.

The app does not diagnose skin cancer, melanoma, or any other condition. It does not score risk, recommend treatment, or replace medical judgment. If you are concerned about a skin change, contact a qualified medical professional.

Users can erase local app data from the app's settings flow where provided, or by deleting the app from the device.

For support or privacy questions, open a support request in the public support repository:

https://github.com/sankalpsthakur/app-support/issues/new

Use the title prefix `[MoleMap]`. Do not include private body photos, sensitive health information, screenshots containing private imagery, payment details, or other confidential information in a public issue.
