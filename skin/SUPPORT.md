# MoleMap Support

For help with MoleMap, open a support request in the public support repository:

https://github.com/sankalpsthakur/app-support/issues/new

Use the title prefix `[MoleMap]` and include:

- Your device model and iOS version
- The app version
- A written description of the issue
- Whether the issue happened during capture, Photos import, local review, export, or settings

Do not include private body photos, sensitive health information, screenshots containing private imagery, or payment details in a public issue. Most support requests can be handled with a written description and device/app version details.

## Privacy

MoleMap is designed as a local-only skin photo journal in the current build. Photos and notes stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them through iOS.

See the MoleMap privacy policy:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sankalpsthakur/app-support/main/skin/PRIVACY.md

## Medical Disclaimer

MoleMap is for personal documentation and education. It does not diagnose, screen, triage, score risk, recommend treatment, or replace medical judgment. If you are concerned about a skin change, contact a qualified medical professional.
