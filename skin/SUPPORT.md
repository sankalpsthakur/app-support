# MoleMap Support

For help with MoleMap, email: sankalphimself@gmail.com

Please do not email private body photos unless you intentionally choose to share them. Most support requests can be handled with:

- Device model and iOS version
- App version
- A written description of the issue
- Screenshots that avoid sensitive imagery

For App Review:

- The app is designed as local-only and offline-capable.
- The app has no account, clinic, visit, upload, analytics, or cloud sync flow in the current build.
- Real-device camera permission testing is required before submission.
