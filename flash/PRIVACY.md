# Recall Cards Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 11, 2026

Recall Cards helps users create and study flashcard decks. The app may store deck titles, prompt text, imported study material, generated cards, review progress, settings, and purchase state.

If AI deck generation is enabled, the topic, prompt, or source material you provide may be sent to Recall Cards servers and AI service providers only to generate study content. Do not submit sensitive personal, medical, financial, or confidential material unless you are comfortable using it for generation.

Recall Cards does not sell personal data. Recall Cards should not use advertising trackers in the release build. App Store privacy answers must match the SDKs and server features included in the submitted build.

Subscription and purchase state is handled through Apple in-app purchase systems. Apple may process purchase identifiers, receipts, and billing information according to Apple's policies.

For support or deletion requests, contact the developer through the support page in this repository.
