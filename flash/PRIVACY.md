# Recall Cards Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 30, 2026

Recall Cards helps users create, import, organize, and study flashcard decks. The app may store deck titles, prompt text, imported study material, generated cards, review progress, settings, and purchase state.

If AI deck generation is enabled, the topic, prompt, or source material you provide may be sent to Recall Cards servers and AI service providers only to generate study content. Do not submit sensitive personal, medical, financial, legal, or confidential material unless you are comfortable using it for generation.

Recall Cards does not sell personal data. The release build should not use advertising trackers. App Store privacy answers must match the SDKs, server features, and data flows included in the submitted build.

Subscription and purchase state is handled through Apple in-app purchase systems. Apple may process purchase identifiers, receipts, and billing information according to Apple's policies.

For support or privacy questions, open a support request in the public support repository:

https://github.com/sankalpsthakur/app-support/issues/new

Use the title prefix `[Recall Cards]`. Do not include private study material, sensitive source text, screenshots with personal information, payment details, or other confidential information in a public issue.
