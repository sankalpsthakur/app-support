# Recall Cards Support

For help with Recall Cards, open a support request in the public support repository:

https://github.com/sankalpsthakur/app-support/issues/new

Use the title prefix `[Recall Cards]` and include:

- Your device model and iOS version
- The app version
- What you were trying to do
- Whether the issue happened while creating, importing, reviewing, or sharing a deck

Do not include private study material, sensitive source text, screenshots with personal information, or payment details in a public issue. If your issue involves private material, describe the problem at a high level without pasting the content.

## Purchases

Recall Cards subscriptions are handled by Apple. You can manage, cancel, or restore subscriptions from your Apple account settings and the app's purchase screen where available.
